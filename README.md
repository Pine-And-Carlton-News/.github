# 📰 Pine & Carlton County News — Development

**Pine & Carlton County News** is a local Minnesota news and public safety network dedicated to covering incidents, fire and EMS responses, weather alerts, and community updates across Pine and Carlton Counties.

This GitHub organization hosts the **source code, backend systems, and automation tools** that power our digital presence — including our website, dispatch automation, and scanner integration services.

---

## 🚀 Projects & Infrastructure
- **Website CMS** – a custom lightweight PHP-based content management system (built without heavy frameworks) for publishing posts and incident updates in real time.  
- **Scanner Integration** – private Icecast-based streams and metadata logging for Pine & Carlton County emergency dispatch.  
- **Automation Tools** – backend services for syncing PulsePoint, Watch Duty, and other public safety APIs.  
- **Admin Dashboard** – internal tools for newsroom management, post publishing, and live call tracking.

---

## 🧰 Tech Stack
- **Language:** PHP 8.2 (no framework, micro-architecture)
- **Database:** MySQL / MariaDB  
- **Hosting:** Hostinger  
- **Streaming:** Icecast  
- **Integrations:** PulsePoint API, Watch Duty, custom metadata proxy  
- **Frontend:** TailwindCSS + minimal Alpine.js  
- **Deployment:** GitHub Actions (planned)

---

## 🔒 Access & Collaboration
This organization is primarily used for **private development** of backend services and automation.  
Certain repositories may be made public for transparency, open data, or community tools as projects mature.

If you’re interested in contributing or collaborating on scanner integrations or local data tools, feel free to reach out via our website or official social channels.
